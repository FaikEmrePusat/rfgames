# Game 2 Brief — Katla-Çiz (name TBD)

Status: **approved for implementation** (product rules locked by user, 2026-10-09).

## Pitch
Players secretly draw parts of one character on a folded vertical paper. When opened, the mismatched figure is the joke/payoff.

## Locked rules
1. Paper is vertical with **4 folds**: kafa, beden, bacaklar, ayak.
2. **2–4 players**. Fewer than 4 → some players draw more than one part, in order.
3. Next player sees **joining guide lines** from the previous part’s bottom edge (peek strip).
4. Draw with whatever the device offers: mouse, finger, stylus.
5. **Online** supported (same social loop as Kapmaca rooms).
6. Final name later; working title **Katla-Çiz**.

## Success criteria
- Fun first reveal on first playtest
- Readable UI on parchment
- Local playable without server; online with Socket room
- Does not regress Kare Kapmaca

## Out of scope (for v1)
- AI auto-draw bots
- Image upload / photo
- More than 4 body sections
- Public matchmaking (code rooms only)
